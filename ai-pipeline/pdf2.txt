conservation. The group is planning an upcoming event to raise awareness about recycling and 
reducing waste. You have some ideas for activities and would like to share them with the group 
leader, Julia. Write an email to Julia. In your email, do the following: • Describe your ideas for 
the awareness event. • Explain why you believe these activities will be effective. • Offer to help 
organize and implement the activities. Write as much as you can and in complete sentences. 
Dear Julia, 
I hope you are doing well. I’m writing to share a few ideas for our upcoming environmental 
awareness event about recycling and reducing waste. I think we could organize three main 
activities: a recycling sorting game, a short student presentation about waste reduction, and a 
table where students can exchange reusable items such as notebooks, bottles, or tote bags. 
These activities would be effective because they are interactive rather than just informative. 
Students are more likely to remember the message if they participate directly and see how 
small daily choices can reduce waste. I would be happy to help prepare the materials, design 
posters, or manage one of the activity tables during the event. 
Best regards, 
Rozhin
